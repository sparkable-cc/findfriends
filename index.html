  1. 1

    Get your friend list

    1. On LinkedIn, go to Settings → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data (help page).
    2. Pick only Connections and request it. LinkedIn emails you a link, usually within minutes.
    3. Drop the ZIP file here. No need to unzip it.
    1. On Facebook, go to Settings → Meta Account → Your information and permissions → Export your information (help page).
    2. Choose Export to device, pick only your friends list, set the format to JSON and start the export.
    3. Drop the ZIP file here. No need to unzip it.

  2. 2

    Sign in

    Use your Sparkable or Bluesky account; it's the same one. Eurosky, Blacksky and others work too.

    You approve in a window from your account's server. This tool can only follow, unfollow and see profiles. It never sees your password.

  3. 3

    Choose who to follow

    Everyone you follow gets a notification: a nice way to say hello. Follows are public.

    Done 🎉

    Tell your friends where to find you. Edit it and post it on LinkedIn or Facebook.

    Come back in a month. New people join every week. Keep your file: next time, people you already follow are set aside, so it's quick.

    All done? Copy your post first if you want it. Finishing clears your contacts and results here and ends this tool's access to your account. Your Sparkable login isn't affected.
